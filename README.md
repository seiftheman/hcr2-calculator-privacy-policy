- **Usage data:** Non-content information about how the app is used, such as feature usage or crash reports.

## Open Source / Transparency

The app may be distributed as open source or may include public source components, depending on the project configuration. This policy is intended to explain the data handling practices of the app itself.

## What Data Is Collected

The app may collect the following information when used:

- Device type and operating system information
- App version and build information
- Locale or language settings
- User-entered calculation inputs and result values
- App preferences or settings
- Crash reports, error logs, and diagnostic information
- Basic usage analytics (for example, feature usage or session timing)

The app does not intentionally collect payment information, government-issued IDs, or highly sensitive personal data unless such data is explicitly provided by the user for a voluntary support or contact request.

---

## How Data Is Used

Collected data may be used to:

- Operate and maintain the app
- Improve accuracy, reliability, and usability
- Diagnose bugs or technical issues
- Understand which features are useful to users
- Respond to support questions or bug reports
- Maintain security and prevent abuse

Calculation inputs are generally processed locally on the user’s device. They are not used for marketing profiles or unrelated third-party analytics unless clearly disclosed in a specific feature.

---

## How Data Is Stored

Data may be stored in the following ways:

- Locally on the user’s device
- In secure cloud storage or analytics providers, if enabled by the app
- In logs or backups for reliability and debugging

The developer uses reasonable safeguards to protect stored information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely risk-free.

---

## Access to Data

Access to personal or usage data is limited to the developer or authorized maintainers of the app, as needed for support, debugging, or service operation.

Data is not sold, rented, or publicly shared in raw form. Aggregated or anonymized statistics may be used for product improvement.
